Terms of Service & Sprint Scope

Last Updated: September 2026. edward@egstudio.tech

By purchasing a sprint on egstudio.tech, you agree to the following terms:

1. White-Label Ownership & IP Transfer

  • Full Transfer Upon Payment: All final exported video deliverables belong 100% to you and your client upon delivery.
  • No Attribution: EG Studio claims no public credit, includes no watermarks, and does not portfolio your client's work without prior written permission.
  • Source Rights: You warrant that you hold all necessary rights and licenses for any raw footage, audio, or assets provided to EG Studio.

2. Footage & Ingestion Limits

  • Baseline Allocation: Each $1,500 sprint covers up to 90 minutes of total raw source footage (Zoom, podcast, iPhone, keynote, or b-roll).
  • Overage: Source footage exceeding 90 minutes per sprint requires separate written approval and a custom ingest quote before editing begins.
  • Deliverables: Deliverables consist of 15 vertical (9:16) videos under 60 seconds each, delivered in three batches of 5 across the 30-day period.

3. Revisions & Approvals

  • Turnaround & Feedback: Up to two revision rounds are included per video. Revision requests must be submitted within 3 business days of batch delivery.
  • Silence as Acceptance: Videos without revision notes after 5 business days are deemed approved to keep the delivery schedule intact.
  • Out-of-Scope Changes: Script changes, new footage submissions after rough-cut approval, or changes to the agreed initial style sample constitute out-of-scope work billed separately.

4. Turnaround & Delays

  • Sprint Clock: The 30-day timeline begins when two criteria are met: (1) Stripe payment clears, and (2) raw assets and creative brief are submitted.
  • Client Pauses: Delays on client feedback exceeding 10 business days will pause the sprint. Sprints paused by the client for more than 30 consecutive days will be delivered as-is with raw project files.

5. Refund Policy

  • Style Sample Guarantee: If the initial style sample delivered in the first 3 days does not align with your brief and revisions cannot resolve it, you may cancel before batch production begins for a 100% refund.
  • Post-Production: Once full production begins following style approval, sprint fees are non-refundable due to dedicated editing labor allocation.

The refund page restates these rules for both SKUs.

6. $300 Entry Offer Scope & Terms

The $300 package is an entry-level test sprint designed for speed and rapid style validation.

  • Deliverables: Exactly 3 vertical (9:16) short-form videos under 60 seconds each.
  • Footage Limit: Source material is limited to a single continuous video file or link (podcast, talk, webinar, or A-roll) not exceeding 20 minutes in total length.
  • Revisions: Includes one (1) round of minor text and pacing revisions across the batch. Revision notes must be submitted within 48 hours of initial delivery.
  • Turnaround: Delivery is completed within 3 to 5 business days after raw footage, assets, and brief requirements are received.
  • Non-Refundable: Due to immediate allocation of editing resources on low-tier orders, the $300 fee is non-refundable once raw files are accepted and production begins.
  • Upgrade Credit: 100% of the $300 fee may be credited toward a full $1,500 Sprint if an upgrade is initiated within 7 days of final delivery.
