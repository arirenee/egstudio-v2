These terms cover the Agency Clip Pack sold at egstudio.tech.

Service Scope

The Agency Clip Pack includes:

  • One source recording (up to 60 minutes)
  • Five short form clips
  • Captions
  • Brand styling using supplied assets
  • Five hook suggestions
  • One revision round

Price and format

The price is $199 per order. Clips are delivered in 9:16 vertical format.

Eligibility

Eligible footage includes podcasts, interviews, webinars, and expert or talking head recordings. The standard pack is for one English language recording with up to two synchronized speakers or cameras.

Delivery

After payment, we share a private project workspace (a Google Drive folder) with the email used for the order. You upload the recording and brand assets there, and we deliver the finished files to the same workspace.

The workspace and the files we deliver are stored in EG Studio’s Google Drive account and cannot be reshared by your team. Download the files and share your own copies with your client. File sharing services may reveal uploader account details if workspace items are shared directly.

What we do not do

  • We do not contact your clients.
  • We do not add EG branding to the delivered files.
  • We do not require timestamps from you.

Ownership

You own the finished clips we deliver to you. You are responsible for having the necessary rights to the recordings, logos, images, music, and other materials you provide.

Delivery timing

The five clips are delivered within 1–3 calendar days once payment clears and all required project materials are ready.

The delivery clock begins when we have a usable recording and any required brand assets in the private project workspace, a completed brief, and any requested NDA agreement is complete. We will notify you if anything is missing before the turnaround period begins.

Revisions

One consolidated revision round is included for caption, branding, and timing corrections.

Payment

Payment is processed by Stripe.
