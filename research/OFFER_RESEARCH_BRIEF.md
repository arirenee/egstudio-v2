# EG Studio Offer Research Brief

## Objective
Identify one new or revised EG Studio offer that can plausibly win a first paying customer faster than the current agency-only positioning.

The research should determine whether EG Studio should broaden from white-label agency fulfillment to a direct, self-serve "raw footage to finished shorts" service for a wider market.

## Strategic constraint
The buying experience should be designed around **no sales call required** unless research shows this creates a material disadvantage. Buyers should be able to understand the service, scope, price, turnaround, revisions, and next step without speaking to sales.

## Buyer segments to examine
Evaluate demand and offer patterns across:
- Creators
- Podcasters
- Consultants
- Coaches
- Founders/executives
- Small businesses
- Brands
- In-house marketing teams
- Agencies

Do not assume all segments deserve separate offers. Look for one offer that can serve several segments without becoming vague.

## Competitive research scope
Study a meaningful set of direct and adjacent competitors. Favor companies with public pricing, public packages, checkout or onboarding flows, customer proof, and recent third-party reviews.

For each competitor capture:
- Company
- URL
- Buyer
- Core promise
- Package name
- Price
- Billing model
- Deliverables
- Turnaround
- Revisions
- Raw/source-footage limits
- Editing features included
- Posting/distribution included or not
- Account/project management included or not
- Sales call required?
- Can a customer buy online immediately?
- Trial/test offer
- Guarantee/refund/cancellation terms
- Trust signals
- Review/customer complaint patterns
- Upsells
- Notable friction
- Observation date
- Source links
- Fact / Hypothesis / Unknown

## Economics
Where data allows, normalize packages into:
- Price per finished short
- Monthly minimum commitment
- Approximate customer wait time
- Number of revisions
- Source-footage allowance

Do not invent labor cost. If EG Studio's actual production cost is unavailable, mark margin as Unknown and state what number is needed.

## Opportunity analysis
Look specifically for gaps such as:
- Forced discovery/sales calls
- Hidden pricing
- High minimum commitments
- Long contracts
- Slow turnaround
- Confusing packages
- Excessive onboarding
- Weak transparency
- Poor review experience
- Limited one-off options
- Expensive entry plans
- Restrictions that frustrate small buyers

## Offer design
Create exactly three candidate offers. Each candidate must specify:
- Name
- Best-fit buyer(s)
- Concrete deliverables
- Price
- Turnaround
- Revision policy
- Raw-footage allowance
- What is excluded
- Purchase/onboarding flow
- Why a buyer would choose it over the researched alternatives
- Main operational risk
- Evidence supporting the price

Score each 1–10 on:
- Existing market demand
- Price/value competitiveness
- Clarity
- No-call/self-serve fit
- Fulfillment simplicity
- Gross-margin potential
- Time to launch
- Likelihood of producing EG Studio's first paying customer

## Final decision
Recommend one offer only.

Use:
**FACT**
**HYPOTHESIS**
**UNKNOWN**

Then state:
- Recommended offer
- Recommended price
- Why now
- Which buyer segments it should target first
- What should remain unchanged on egstudio.tech
- What should change only after approval
- The smallest real-world test to validate demand
- Kill criteria

Do not modify the live site during this research task.
