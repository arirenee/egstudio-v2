Refund Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026. This policy covers EG Studio's current Episode-to-Shorts offer and any legacy Starter or Sprint order accepted through an EG Studio checkout. Refund requests go to ops@egstudio.tech.

Episode-to-Shorts — $199 one time

  • Before production: Contact EG Studio as soon as possible if you need to cancel before production begins.
  • Unsuitable source: If EG Studio determines before production that the submitted recording cannot reasonably support five useful clips, you may choose a full refund or a reduced scope with the unavailable portion refunded.
  • After production begins: The order is non-refundable once EG Studio begins source review/editing for production, except for an agreed refund of unfinished scope when EG Studio cannot complete the order for reasons within our control.
  • Revisions: The included consolidated revision round is a service remedy, not a new project brief. A new creative direction, replacement moment selection or new source footage is outside the included revision round.

Delivery timing

The 3–5 business day delivery window begins after payment clears and EG Studio has a complete brief plus usable source files. Delays caused by inaccessible, missing, corrupted or materially changed customer files pause the delivery clock. If EG Studio misses the stated delivery window for reasons within our control, contact ops@egstudio.tech so we can agree on a revised delivery date or refund the unfinished portion of the order.

Legacy Starter and Sprint orders

Legacy $300 3-Clip Starter and $1,500 Sprint orders remain governed by the refund terms presented when those orders were purchased. EG Studio will honor those existing terms for already-paid orders.

How to request a refund or cancellation

Email ops@egstudio.tech from the email used for the order and identify the project. When a refund is due under this policy, it is returned through the applicable payment method or payment processor.

Questions

If you are unsure whether production has started or which policy applies to your order, contact ops@egstudio.tech before sending additional project materials.
