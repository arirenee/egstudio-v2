Refund Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026.

This policy covers the EG Studio Agency Clip Pack.

Before production begins

You may request cancellation before editing begins. Eligible cancellations are refunded in full.

A cancellation is eligible when you email ops@egstudio.tech before we start reviewing your recording for editing.

If the recording is not a fit

We’ll let you know before editing begins and issue a refund. This refund is for the full amount paid.

After production begins

Orders become non-refundable once editing work has started.

If EG Studio cannot finish your order for reasons within our control, we will agree with you on a refund of the unfinished work.

If we miss the 1–3 calendar day delivery window for reasons within our control, we will agree with you on a revised delivery date or refund the unfinished portion of the order.

Missing, inaccessible, corrupted, or materially changed files pause the delivery clock.

The included revision round corrects the delivered clips. A new direction, replacement moments, or a new recording is outside that round.

Need help?

Email:

ops@egstudio.tech

Send refund or cancellation requests from the email used for the order and identify the project. Refunds due under this policy are returned through the applicable payment method or payment processor.
