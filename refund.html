Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026. This policy covers EG Studio's $300 3-Clip Starter and $1,500 Sprint. Refund requests go to ops@egstudio.tech.

$300 3-Clip Starter — 3 Videos

  • Before production: Contact EG Studio as soon as possible if you need to cancel before production begins.
  • After production begins: The $300 fee is non-refundable once raw files are accepted and production begins.
  • Upgrade credit: 100% of the $300 fee is credited toward a full $1,500 Sprint if the upgrade is initiated within 7 days of final delivery.

$1,500 Sprint — 15 Videos

  • Style sample: An initial style sample is delivered in the first 3 days of the Sprint workflow.
  • Before batch production: If the style sample does not align with the brief and the included revision process cannot resolve the issue, you may cancel before batch production begins for a 100% refund.
  • After style approval: Once full production begins following style approval, Sprint fees are non-refundable.

How to request a refund or cancellation

Email ops@egstudio.tech from the email used for the order and identify the project. EG Studio handles project, cancellation and billing matters with the agency purchaser—not through the agency's client.

When a refund is due under this policy, it is returned through the applicable payment method or payment processor.

Questions

If you are unsure whether production has started or which policy applies to your order, contact ops@egstudio.tech before sending additional project materials.
