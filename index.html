FAQ

Who is this built for?

Agencies already producing client podcasts, interviews, webinars or thought-leadership recordings and needing short-form editing capacity behind the scenes.

What do I need to send?

A shareable source-footage link, any brand assets you want used, and one reference that shows the pacing or caption style you want.

What is not included?

Filming, posting, full-episode editing, complex custom animation and native project files are outside these fixed offers unless separately agreed.

What are the revision limits?

The three-video test includes one minor text-and-pacing revision round across the batch. The full Sprint includes up to two revision rounds per video.

What happens immediately after I pay?

Stripe redirects you to the project brief. Submit the source link and brand direction there; ops@egstudio.tech handles the project from that point forward.
