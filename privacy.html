Privacy

EG Studio uses the information you provide only to process payment, receive project materials, run the project and deliver the editing service.

Your client stays your client. EG Studio is a behind-the-scenes fulfillment partner. We do not contact, solicit, market to, invoice, or build a relationship with your client.

What white-label means here

Your agency owns the client relationship. EG Studio works behind the scenes as your production partner.

  • We do not email, message or otherwise contact your client.
  • We do not place EG Studio branding on delivered videos or delivery files.
  • We do not pitch or market additional services to your client.
  • We do not add client work to our portfolio without prior written permission.
  • We do not ask your client for testimonials, referrals or introductions.

You send the source material and project direction to us. We return the finished work to your agency.

Information used to run the project

  • EG Studio collects Stripe checkout identity, questionnaire answers, brand assets and footage links as needed to fulfill the order.
  • EG Studio does not sell customer or client lists.
  • Card numbers stay with Stripe.
  • Access or deletion requests can be sent to ops@egstudio.tech.

Client-work publicity

Your client's content is not used as EG Studio portfolio or promotional material without prior written permission.
