Privacy

Last updated: October 1, 2026. This page explains the information EG Studio uses when you visit the site, request a readiness review, purchase an order or send project materials.

Information we collect

  • Business and contact information: work email, agency website and information you choose to include in a readiness review, project brief or other submission.
  • Project materials: recording links, footage, brand assets, creative direction and related project information you provide for evaluation or production.
  • Payment and order information: checkout identity, payment status, order amount and transaction identifiers supplied through Stripe. Card numbers are processed by Stripe and are not stored by EG Studio.
  • Website and analytics information: pages viewed, site events, referral or campaign information, and browser/device information collected through standard site logs and analytics or advertising tools.

How we use information

  • To receive and evaluate readiness-review submissions.
  • To process orders, receive project materials, edit and deliver purchased work.
  • To send transactional confirmations and project communications.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website and project workflow.
  • To understand site usage and measure marketing or campaign performance.

Service providers

EG Studio uses third-party services to operate the business. Depending on how you use the site, information may be processed by services including:

  • Stripe for payment processing.
  • Supabase for project intake and related workflow data.
  • Resend for transactional email.
  • Google Drive when used for project files or delivery.
  • PostHog for site analytics and product/event measurement.
  • Pinterest for advertising and campaign measurement where the Pinterest tag is active.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. EG Studio does not sell customer or client lists.

Client recordings and white-label work

EG Studio works at the agency's direction. We do not use a client's recording or finished work as EG Studio portfolio or promotional material without prior written permission.

Unless the agency instructs us otherwise in writing, we do not contact the agency's client, invoice the client or market EG Studio services directly to the client.

Retention

Information is retained as reasonably necessary to operate projects, maintain business and transaction records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Project materials may also remain in connected project services while they are needed for evaluation, production or delivery.

Your choices and requests

You may request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided by contacting ops@egstudio.tech. Some transaction or business records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business, accounting or legal purposes.

Browser privacy controls, content blockers and similar settings may limit some analytics or advertising measurement.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to ops@egstudio.tech.
